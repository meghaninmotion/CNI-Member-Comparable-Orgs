# CNI-Member-Comparable-Orgs
A multi-tab exploration of CNI members' commitment to 6 comparable organizations

This visualization represents how CNI's 229 members engage with comparable member organizations, where exploration of relationships of overlap clusters and breakdown of organizations within membership at those comparable organizations may be of value. The comparable member organizations include ARL, CLIR, CSG, DLF, ERN, and SPARC; additional notes about EDUCAUSE, NDSA, Jisc, and RLUK are noted.  

This comparison has demonstrated that CNI members may also be members of these comparable organizations, and therefore this view may demonstrate the complexity and requirements CNI members face when engaging with a wider range of organizations - to meet their needs, maintain professional standards, share their work, and expand their knowledge. Data were gathered from these organizations' websites and used as the basis for several complementary visualizations that surface more comprehensive member experience details.

More details on CNI's 229 member organizations can be found at https://www.cni.org/about-cni/membership/members.

This visualization was developed by pairing with Claude AI to develop html to represent the annual schedule of meetings and events for this group of organizations. It is presented for exploration.
